CONSERVATION AUDIT

15 MW energy-consistency check

PHYSICS BOUND

A 15 MW source has an absolute annual electricity ceiling of 131.4 GWh. Hydrogen stoichiometry alone therefore imposes a hard upper bound on methanol production; adding DAC, synthesis, water and balance-of-plant lowers it further.

Thesis reported51,058 t/y
